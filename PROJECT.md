# Project

## Purpose
A professional company services site for websites, apps, games, interactive design and campaigns. Explain deliverables, process and contact without experimental projects or invented credentials.

## Direction
Primary surface: Decide / Learn. Preserve Studio typography, serif emphasis, warm paper, ink and coral. Asymmetric editorial layout with a static geometric brand composition. No product mockups, lab widgets, personal portfolio, fake clients, testimonials or metrics. Design self-audit: 0/10.

## Scope
Responsive service disclosures, concrete deliverables, three-step scoping/design/build process, mobile navigation with Escape and link-close behavior, direct email and an honest brief builder. Self-hosted font, metadata and static build. Experimental content and its JavaScript/CSS are removed, not hidden. Standalone projects remain untouched.

## Contact and privacy
No backend, tracking or email sends. Notes stay in browser memory. Native mailto values use encodeURIComponent and CRLF. Visitors review and send drafts themselves.

## Regression protection
Public asset/source checks prohibit experimental project and preview machinery. Browser suites cover contact drafts with native decoding, disclosures, service-specific inquiries, mobile navigation, anchors, accessibility, reduced motion, no-JavaScript fallback, errors and overflow.
