# Project

## Purpose

Help someone with an idea understand the services, try a small sample of interaction design, and start a practical project conversation.

## Direction

Primary surface: Decide / Learn. Editorial rather than generic software marketing. Large sans headlines with restrained serif emphasis, thin rules, asymmetric composition, warm paper and ink, coral for action and play. No stock imagery or fabricated portfolio.

## Scope delivered

- Responsive homepage for websites, apps, campaign creative and interactive experiences.
- Pointer, touch and keyboard-responsive geometric composition.
- Four accessible preview toggles, a checklist prototype, keyboard/touch dot game and headline experiment.
- Native service disclosures and concrete process explanation.
- Internal Snake demo link, explicitly not client work.
- Honest email brief builder and direct verified contact link.
- Mobile navigation with Escape and link-close behaviour.
- Reduced-motion support, focus indicators, semantic landmarks and 44px controls.
- Self-hosted font, favicon, social image, canonical metadata, robots and sitemap.
- Static build, security headers, GitHub repository and Vercel production deployment.

## Privacy and security

No credentials in source. No remote font request, third-party JavaScript, tracking, backend or email sends. Form values stay in memory in the visitor's browser until they choose to open their mail app. No claim of successful email delivery.

## Design self-audit

Slop score: 0/10. No tech gradient, indigo default, equal feature tiles, accent rails, blur, fake stats, icon toppers, centred-stack composition or default Inter typography. This is a Decide / Learn layout. The geometric lab is an actual user-controlled experiment, not a fake product screenshot.
